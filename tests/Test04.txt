--- 24
QUESTION: Monica says that most people who buy her book

QUESTION: How did Monica's parents feel about her early interest in gardening?
A: They were concerned about the effects on her health.

QUESTION: Why did Monica give up her job on a magazine?

B: They are aimed at amateur enthusiasts.
